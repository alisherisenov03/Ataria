# ATARIA — Instagram content brief

Running brief for the ATARIA AT-SM23 stand mixer (kitchen machine) Instagram account.
Collected from the first long working chat (Sep 22 – Oct 1, 2026). Read it before any
new content task and reuse the references below instead of asking the user again.

## Working rules

- Talk to the user in **Russian**. Write image/video prompts in **English**. On-screen text
  and voice-over in Russian (or Kazakh if asked).
- Market: **Kazakhstan**, sales on **Kaspi.kz** and WhatsApp. Free delivery across Kazakhstan.
- The user generates video on higgsfield.ai: **Seedance 2.5, 720p, 9:16** (their plan).
  I prepare keyframes (gpt_image_2_5, high, 2k) + prompts; the user animates; I edit,
  add audio and assemble (ffmpeg in the Higgsfield sandbox).
- **Never AI-edit or regenerate an old approved photo.** Quality is lost. Generate fresh
  from real reference photos, or do precise manual pixel fixes on the chosen image.
- All text and digits on the product must be exact. Check every generated frame:
  logo, icon pills, LED + buttons, dial, bowl handle, hook shape.
- Do not put Kaspi button / warranty / installment on feed posts — only in Stories.
- People must look real (not AI-smooth). If a person is in frame they wear the ATARIA apron.
- UGC/recipe videos: shot-from-phone feel, hands, no ad end-card.

## Style — what "дорого" means for this account

**Default = "европейский стиль": expensive minimal European, quiet luxury.**
- Light background: white, warm plaster, light stone/travertine, light wood. The dark
  graphite mixer is the contrast. Needed to balance the feed, which is currently too dark.
- Soft window daylight, muted noble colours, lots of empty space, simple composition.
- NO flashes, glow, haze, lens flares, backlight beams, bright saturated colours.
- Kitchen should feel real and achievable in Kazakhstan — expensive but not a showroom
  island villa ("не остров").
- Camera: calm, slow, few moves. Audio: calm, warm ambient, no beats/drops, piano quiet.
- Narrative like BORK reels: ingredients → product macro detail → process → the result
  being enjoyed by a person. Vary angles and shot sizes so it is not monotonous.
- Secondary style (used for the "О товаре" highlight and the first carousel): dark
  charcoal "quiet luxury", black marble with gold veins, soft light, Montserrat typography
  (small gold label, large white title, grey subline).

## Product facts (ATARIA AT-SM23)

- 2000 W, planetary mixing, overheating protection, suction feet underneath.
- 7 L stainless-steel bowl with **two side handles only**: a thin chrome rod loop that
  sags in an arc just under the rim, fixed on two small plates with 4 rivets each
  (see `refs/real_4views_bowl_handle.jpg`). Never mug-style handles, never 4 handles.
- Clear splash-guard lid with a pouring chute.
- 6 speeds + turbo P. LED display with blue digits (timer up to 15 min; show 15:00),
  buttons **TIME, +, −** in that order under the digits.
- Chrome dial (mirror bezel, knurled edge, protrudes ~3 cm): circled marks
  P(−34°), 0, 1(39°), 2(75°), 3(111°), 4(148°), 5(184°), 6(219°), thin arc from 1 to 6,
  diamond pointer on the knob. When turning, **only the knob + diamond rotate; the
  printed marks stay still**. Blue ring lights up when running (0 = off).
- ATARIA wordmark on the head (A letters notched, no crossbar), 3 icon pills on the right
  (1-2 / 1-max / 1-max). Lock lever with padlock icon on the column.
- Grey-graphite matte plastic body. Base under the bowl is long (as long as the head).
- Shown straight/side-on with controls to camera — the head-front grinder cover is not
  visible in that view. Top cover has lines + downward triangle.
- Dough hook: simple J — disk on top, one rod, one curve (NOT a spiral), reaches near the
  bottom of the 7 L bowl. Also flat beater and whisk.
- Attachments: meat grinder (satin aluminium, large metal tray, 3 plates fine/medium/coarse,
  sausage + kebbe nozzles), glass blender jug **1.5 L**, vegetable slicer (clear housing,
  3 drums: coarse, fine, slicing), dough roller (8 thickness settings, ~20 cm), noodle
  cutter (~5 mm strips). Roller/cutter/grinder screw into the front port of the head.
- ~12 kg with all attachments. 12-month warranty (factory defects).

## Technical notes

- Local machine cannot reach Higgsfield CDNs or upload.higgsfield.ai; do downloads,
  compositing and uploads inside `sandbox_exec` (16k-char command limit, 120 s timeout,
  use a `background` keepalive like `sleep 1500` to keep files between calls).
- The sandbox refuses base64 blobs of images; bring files in by URL. User-provided local
  files go through `media_upload_widget`.
- Previews: print a <9.5 KB base64 JPEG after a unique tag and decode it locally from the
  session transcript.
- seedance_2_5 does not support `use_unlim` via MCP (35 credits / 5 s at 720p).
