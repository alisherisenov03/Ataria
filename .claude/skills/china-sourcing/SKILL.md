# China sourcing

Landed cost per unit = factory price (¥ × rate) + cargo (kg × $/kg × rate, e.g. 1.5–1.6 $/kg) + packaging + Almaty–Astana leg, split by boxes/weight like the cabinet purchase batches.
Reference batches: SM23 200 pcs, 115 500 ¥, deposit 30% then 70%, cargo est. 2 380 kg × 1.5 $; pillows 320 pcs, logistics 856 $ total.
Lead time: SM23 55 days production + 25 shipping; pillows 15–20 + 25. Deposit must be paid lead time + 7 days before stock runs out.
Compare at least 3 suppliers: price, MOQ, certificates, sample, reviews/years on platform. Then run kaspi-unit-economics at the expected Kaspi price. Payments are Alisher's decision only.
