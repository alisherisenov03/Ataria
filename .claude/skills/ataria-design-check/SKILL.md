## Host consistency (SM23 reels)
The host is always "Aigerim" (brand/sm23-host-aigerim.md, character sheet job f67cdeca-a2d7-477f-8873-df1e8e96bed9). Check: same face, low bun, cream rib-knit sweater, beige linen apron, gold stud earrings, thin gold ring on the right hand, jeans, white sneakers. Any drift = regenerate with the character sheet as the first reference.

## Brand and format
- Warm natural light, homely Kazakh or European interior, film grain, no glossy stock look.
- Reels 9:16, text in Russian, large and readable on a phone, contrast passes.
- One clear message per frame.

## How to check
Download the result, look at it next to the reference (side by side), list differences. Show Alisher only frames that pass; say in one line what was rejected and why.
